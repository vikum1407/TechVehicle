2. Vehicle Owner Features OWNER

🚗Add Vehicle

Registration no., make/model/year, fuel type, vehicle type, current mileage.

📊Vehicle Dashboard

Quick actions: Log Fuel, Add Service, Add Expense, Insights.

⛽Log Fuel

Odometer + litres + cost. This is what powers all the smart predictions.

🔧Add Service Record

Tap-to-select categories, parts & brand, cost, receipt photos.

💰Add Expense

Insurance, revenue licence, fines, parking, tolls, and more.

📜History

Searchable timeline of every service, fuel, and expense record.

📈Insights

Charts for mileage growth, fuel efficiency, and spending trends.

🔮Predictions

"Oil change due soon" style alerts, plus an upcoming-cost forecast.

🧪Vehicle Tests

Emission test, wheel alignment, and chain service tracking.

📚Knowledge Hub

Manufacturer specs — compare your own records against them.

⏰Renewal Reminders

Alerts before your Revenue Licence or Emission Test expires.

📄Export History

Download your full vehicle history as a styled PDF.

3. Sharing, Booking & Selling OWNER

  • Share with a garage — pick exactly which service records a garage can see before your visit.
  • Book an appointment — search a garage, pick a date & time slot, message them directly in-app.
  • Sell / Transfer a vehicle — enter the buyer's phone number, all history moves over once they accept.
  • Family sharing — give another phone number (e.g. a family member) access to a vehicle.

4. Garage / Service Center Features GARAGE

🏭Register Garage

Name, address, and an optional Business Registration (BR) number.

🗓️Schedule

Weekly work days, time slots, and calendar overrides for holidays.

📅Bookings

Confirm requests, or suggest a different date/time.

👁️Shared Records

See what an owner shared with you before their appointment.

✅Submit Completed Work

Parts, brand, cost, and category — sent to the owner for approval.

🚶Walk-in Service

Log a job for a vehicle with no prior booking, by registration number.

👥Customers

Every vehicle you've served, with reminders for overdue ones.

📖Customer Ledger

Full itemized job history per customer, like a digital service book.

6. Account & Language EVERYONE

  • Profile — your photo, activity stats, and current role.
  • Language — switch between English, Sinhala, and Tamil anytime in Settings.
  • Log out — signs out this device only.
  • Log out of all devices — signs out everywhere at once. Use this if your phone is ever lost or stolen.
