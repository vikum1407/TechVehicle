# Vocksy — Everything the App Can Do

*A simple guide for vehicle owners and garages. No technical terms — just what each feature does and how it helps you.*

---

## Getting Started

### Signing In
You don't need a password. Just enter your mobile number, and we send you a one-time code (OTP) by SMS. Enter the code and you're in. The next time you open the app, you're already signed in.

### Choosing Your Role
The first time you sign in, you'll choose whether you're a **Vehicle Owner** or a **Garage / Service Center**. You can also register a garage later from your profile, even if you started as a vehicle owner — many garage owners use both.

## For Vehicle Owners

### Adding Your Vehicle
Enter your registration number, make, model, year, fuel type, and current mileage. You can add a photo too. If you already have some service history, you'll be guided through a short set of simple questions (like "when did you last change your engine oil?") so your vehicle profile isn't starting from zero.

### Your Vehicle Dashboard
This is the home screen for each vehicle — your current mileage, a summary of what's coming up, and quick buttons to log fuel, add a service, add an expense, or see your analytics.

### Logging Fuel Fill-ups
Every time you fill up, log your odometer reading, how much fuel you put in, and the cost. This is the single most important habit for the app — the more regularly you log your mileage, the more accurate everything else becomes (predictions, cost estimates, fuel economy).

### Adding a Service Record
When you get work done — an oil change, brake pads, anything — log what was done, the cost, the mileage at the time, and which brand of parts were used if you know. You can attach photos of the receipt or the old part. Some services (like an oil change or tyre change) ask a couple of extra questions so the app can track things like your actual oil grade or how long your tyres are lasting.

### Tracking Expenses
Log anything else that costs money on the vehicle — insurance, registration/revenue licence, fines, parking, tolls, accessories, or washing. Everything is dated and categorised so you can see exactly where your money goes.

### Vehicle History
A searchable timeline of everything you've logged — services, expenses, and fuel — with filters by date, category, or mileage range. You can also export your full history as a PDF, which is useful when selling the vehicle or showing a mechanic your past records.

### Analytics
A visual breakdown of your vehicle's costs — how much you've spent overall, split by category (service, legal/compliance, fuel), your cost per kilometre, your fuel efficiency trend, and a quick "Trip Ready" check that tells you at a glance whether anything urgent needs attention before a long journey.

### Predictions
Based on your mileage and service history, the app tells you what's likely due soon or already overdue — like "your engine oil is due in 500 km" — and explains why, citing either the manufacturer's recommendation or your own past pattern.

### Cost Forecast
An estimate of how much you're likely to spend over the next few months, based on what's coming due and what similar services have cost you before.

### Vehicle Tests
A dedicated place to track things that need periodic renewal or testing:
- **Emission Test** — log your results and get reminders before your next renewal
- **Wheel Alignment** — for cars/vans
- **Chain Service** — for motorcycles and three-wheelers
- **Insurance** and **Revenue Licence** — the app reminds you before these expire, starting a month ahead

### Knowledge Hub
A searchable reference for common vehicle makes and models — recommended oil grade, tyre size, service intervals, and known issues. If it's your own vehicle, it also compares what you're actually using against what the manufacturer recommends and flags any mismatch.

### Notifications
You'll get a notification when a service is due, a renewal is approaching, someone shares or sells you a vehicle, a garage responds to your booking, or a garage submits completed work for your approval.

### Sharing History with a Garage
Before or during a garage visit, you can share a few of your recent service records with them — so they know your vehicle's history without you having to explain it. You choose exactly what to share; nothing is shared automatically. Personal expenses like fines or parking are never included in anything you share.

### Booking a Garage Appointment
Search for a registered garage, pick an available date and time slot, and confirm your booking. You can optionally attach recent service history to the booking. The garage can confirm your slot or suggest a different time if needed.

### Selling or Transferring a Vehicle
When you sell your vehicle, enter the buyer's mobile number. They get a request to accept the full verified history — every service, expense, and fuel record — which then transfers completely to their account and is removed from yours. This is what makes the vehicle's history trustworthy: it travels with the vehicle, not just with you.

Before accepting, a buyer can preview a summary of the vehicle's condition and spending — without seeing anything you'd consider private.

### Family / Shared Access
You can give a family member view-only access to a vehicle — useful if more than one person drives it or you want to keep them in the loop.

### Three-Wheeler Daily Trip Log
If you drive a three-wheeler for income, you can log your daily start/end odometer reading, fuel used, and earnings — the app works out your cost per kilometre and daily profit automatically.

### Your Profile
See a summary of your account (vehicles, services, fuel logs, expenses logged), update your profile photo, add an email address (used for account recovery), switch the app's language, and log out of all your devices at once if you ever lose your phone.

### Language
The app currently supports **English** and **Sinhala** throughout, with a small but growing set of **Tamil** translations. You can switch language anytime from Settings.

## For Garages & Service Centers

### Registering Your Garage
Add your garage name, location, and Business Registration (BR) number if you have one. Garages with a BR number get a "verified" badge that owners can see when deciding who to share history with or book with — but it's not required to use the app.

### Setting Your Schedule
Choose which days you're open, how many vehicles you can take per day, and your available time slots. You can also override specific dates — mark a public holiday, a fully-booked day, or leave a note for customers.

### Managing Bookings
See every booking as it comes in — confirm it, suggest a different time if needed, message the customer directly, or cancel if something changes. If the owner attached their service history, you can see it before the appointment.

### Calendar View
A month-at-a-glance view of how booked each day is, with any special notices you've set clearly shown.

### Submitting Completed Work
After finishing a job, fill in what was done, parts used, brands, and the cost. This gets sent to the owner, who reviews and accepts it into their permanent vehicle history — building the same kind of trustworthy record for them.

### Logging a Walk-in Customer
For customers who show up without booking through the app, you can log the completed service directly against their vehicle (if they're registered) without needing a prior booking.

### Customers
See every vehicle you've served, how many jobs, total revenue from them, and when they're next due for a service — with a one-tap reminder you can send.

### Revenue & Business Insights
A visual breakdown of your garage's business — total and monthly revenue, average job value, how many customers come back, and which types of jobs bring in the most money.

### Customer Ledger
A detailed, itemised service book per customer and vehicle type — the same idea as the paper ledger many garages already keep, just organised for you automatically.

### Ratings & Reviews
Customers can rate and leave feedback after a completed job, which builds your visible reputation over time.

## A Few Things Worth Knowing

- **Nothing is shared without your say-so.** History sharing with a garage, family member, or buyer is always something you actively choose — never automatic.
- **Personal expenses stay personal.** Categories like fines, parking, and tolls are never shown to anyone but you — not a garage, not a buyer, not a family member — even though they're still part of your own full records.
- **Predictions are guidance, not orders.** Every prediction explains where it comes from (manufacturer spec, or your own history) so you can judge it yourself.
- **There's no dispute button.** If a garage's submitted work doesn't match what happened, the app expects that to be sorted out directly between you and the garage — the app just doesn't save it to your history until you accept it.
