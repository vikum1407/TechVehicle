Privacy Policy

How Vocksy handles your data

Effective: 28 August 2026 Last updated: 28 August 2026
Vocksy is a vehicle maintenance tracking app. We collect only what is needed to provide the service. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can control it. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated as you use the app.

Account Information
  • Phone number — used for account creation and login via OTP verification. We do not store passwords.
  • Role — whether you are a Vehicle Owner or a Garage / Service Center.
Vehicle Information
  • Registration number, make, model, year, fuel type, and vehicle type
  • Purchase date, owner count, and vehicle notes (optional)
  • Revenue licence expiry and emission test expiry dates
  • Vehicle photos
  • Current odometer reading and mileage history
Service & Maintenance Records
  • Service records: date, category, description, mileage, cost, parts used, brands
  • Structured data for specific service types: oil grade, tyre size, emission test readings (CO%, HC ppm, CO2%, Lambda), AC refrigerant details
  • Fuel logs: date, odometer reading, litres, cost
  • Expense records: date, category, amount, description
  • Receipt and bill photos uploaded by you
Garage Information (Garage accounts only)
  • Garage name, Business Registration (BR) number (optional), contact details
  • Availability schedule, time slots, and calendar settings
Usage Data
  • Booking records, share sessions, and service submissions between owners and garages
  • Push notification tokens (for sending reminders and alerts)
  • App version and device type (collected by Expo / Firebase)

2 How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data used
Authenticate your account Phone number, OTP
Provide vehicle maintenance tracking Vehicle data, service records, fuel logs, expenses
Generate service predictions and reminders Mileage, service history, manufacturer interval data
Send push notifications Push token, notification preferences

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third-party marketing, or any purpose beyond operating the Vocksy service.

3 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following service providers who operate under strict data processing agreements:

Provider Purpose Data shared
Google Firebase Phone number authentication, push notifications Phone number, push notification token
Neon (PostgreSQL) Database hosting All app data stored in the database
Cloudflare R2 Photo and file storage Uploaded photos and receipt images
Expo (EAS) Mobile app delivery App bundle, device info
Render Backend server hosting API requests processed by the backend

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Sharing between users

When you choose to share service records with a garage, only the records you select are shared. The garage sees a read-only view. You control what is shared and can stop sharing at any time.

4 Storage & Security

Your data is stored on servers located in the Asia Pacific region. We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and encrypted database storage.

Photos and receipt images are compressed to approximately 200–400 KB before storage to minimise your storage footprint and cost.

Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using the platform's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). We do not store your OTP codes.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We take reasonable precautions but cannot guarantee absolute security.

5 Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — view all your vehicle records, service history, fuel logs, and expenses within the app at any time.
  • Export — generate a PDF of your full service and expense history from the History screen.
  • Correction — edit any record directly within the app.
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at vikum1407@gmail.com.

6 Vehicle History Transfer

Vocksy includes a Sell / Transfer feature that allows you to transfer your vehicle's complete history to a buyer. When a transfer is accepted:

  • All vehicle records are permanently removed from your account
  • The full history is added to the buyer's account
  • The transfer is irreversible once the buyer accepts

Until the buyer accepts, the transfer remains pending and you retain full access. This design ensures the vehicle history is tamper-evident and trustworthy for the buyer.

7 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, we will delete all associated personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

Anonymised, aggregated data (with no personal identifiers) may be retained for service improvement purposes.

8 Children's Privacy

Vocksy is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, for material changes, notify you via a push notification or in-app notice.

Continued use of Vocksy after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
